# Dashbite — W1.01 Baseline Scope & Ownership

**Date:** 10/28/2026  
**Owner:** Cyrel

## 1. User Roles
- Customer
- Restaurant
- Rider
- Admin

## 2. MVP Capabilities

**Customer**
- Browse restaurants and menus
- Add items to cart
- Checkout with address, delivery fee, and payment choice
- Track order status (no continuous live GPS)
- View order history
- Rate restaurant and rider separately after delivery

**Restaurant**
- Manage menu and item availability
- Accept or reject orders
- Mark orders as preparing / ready
- Enter phone, message, or walk-in orders

**Rider**
- Go online/offline
- Share location while online
- Receive 1 km ring offers
- Accept one delivery
- Update pickup and delivery status
- Record COD amount collected

**Admin**
- Approve or disable accounts
- Set delivery fee and service boundary
- Monitor ratings, attendance, and complaints
- Review payment/settlement records

**Dispatch**
- Simultaneous 1 km ring blasts
- First valid acceptance wins
- Expand ring by 1 km after timeout
- Stop at service boundary with "No rider available"

**Payments**
- Record COD and online payments
- Keep COD remittance and settlement traceable

## 3. Out of Scope / Deferred
- Continuous live GPS tracking
- Ratings or attendance affecting dispatch priority
- Unapproved online payment provider or commission
- Any new feature request outside this list → log as deferred
